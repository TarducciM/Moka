What Moka does to your PC

  • It asks Windows not to go to sleep and, if you choose so, not to turn off the screen.
  • Only if you choose so, on laptops: it changes the “When I close the lid” setting for as long as needed and always puts it back as it was. If you change it yourself in the meantime, Moka respects your choice.
  • Only if you choose so: at the end of a session it turns off the screen, locks, sleeps, hibernates or shuts down the PC, always after a countdown you can cancel.
